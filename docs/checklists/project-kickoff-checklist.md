# Project Kickoff Checklist

Use this checklist when running the first formal meeting for a newly approved project. Complete all items before or during the kickoff. The meeting itself marks the official transition from Initiation to Planning.

---

## Pre-reads (distribute at least 2 business days before the meeting)

- [ ] Project One-pager / Charter reviewed and shared
- [ ] High-level timeline and milestone map distributed
- [ ] Stakeholder list shared with attendees
- [ ] Initial risk list included in pre-read packet
- [ ] Meeting invite sent with agenda attached
- [ ] Relevant background docs (PRDs, research, comparable projects) linked

---

## Meeting Agenda (suggested 60–90 minutes)

| # | Topic | Owner | Duration |
|---|-------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Welcome & introductions | PM | 5 min |
| 2 | Project overview — problem, goals, success metrics | PdM / Product Lead | 10 min |
| 3 | Scope, non-scope, and constraints | PM | 10 min |
| 4 | Roles & responsibilities (RACI walkthrough) | PM | 10 min |
| 5 | High-level timeline and key milestones | PM | 10 min |
| 6 | Initial risk discussion | PM + Team | 10 min |
| 7 | Working agreements (branching, PR conventions, ceremonies) | Tech Lead / PM | 10 min |
| 8 | Open Q&A | All | 10 min |
| 9 | Next steps and owners | PM | 5 min |

---

## During the Meeting

- [ ] Record decisions in the [Decision Log](../templates/decision-log-template.md)
- [ ] Capture open questions / parking-lot items with owners
- [ ] Agree on sprint/iteration cadence and ceremony schedule
- [ ] Confirm communication channels (Slack, email lists, project board URL)
- [ ] Confirm Definition of Ready and Definition of Done (or schedule a follow-up)

---

## Outputs (complete within 2 business days after meeting)

- [ ] Meeting notes published and distributed
- [ ] Decision log initialized with kickoff decisions
- [ ] [Risk Register](../templates/risk-register-template.md) created with initial risks
- [ ] Project board/backlog skeleton created
- [ ] Sprint/ceremony calendar invites sent
- [ ] Working agreements documented in the repo (e.g., `CONTRIBUTING.md` or `docs/`)

---

## Entry Criteria (before kickoff can be scheduled)

- Project One-pager approved by Product Lead / Sponsor
- Team roster confirmed (PM, PdM, Devs, QA, and any additional roles)
- Project board or repository created

## Exit Criteria (kickoff is complete when)

- All attendees understand project goals, scope, and their roles
- Initial backlog created (even if not fully estimated)
- Communication rhythm established
- At least one sprint/iteration planned or scheduled

---

## RACI

| Artifact | Author | Approver | Consumers |
|----------|--------|----------|-----------|
| Kickoff Checklist | PM | PdM | All team members |
| Meeting Notes | PM | PdM | All stakeholders |
